Shiftly

Your shifts, in your pocket

Plan your roster, repeat your shift pattern and compare schedules with people you choose.

Scan the QR code to get started

wiggli.github.io/Shiftly/

iPhone

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install Shiftly, or use the browser menu → Add to Home screen → Install.
  3. Open the new Shiftly icon.

Open Shiftly from its new icon and sign in with Google for your own private calendar. Scanning this code does not give anyone access to another person’s roster. Invite someone separately through More → People & access.
